Questions

Good to know.

What is Playstoria?

Playstoria is a community store where people discover and download apps, games and software made by Ethiopian developers — and where developers publish their work for free.

Is it free to use?

Yes. Browsing and downloading free apps costs nothing, and you don't need an account. Developers can submit unlimited projects without an upload fee.

Can I sell my app?

Yes. Set a price in ETB when you upload. Buyers pay by Telebirr, an admin verifies the payment, and you keep 90% of each approved sale. You can request a payout from the Earnings tab once you reach 100 ETB.

What can I upload?

Android APKs, PC games and software (ZIP, 7z, RAR, EXE, MSI, DMG, AppImage), browser games packaged as a ZIP with an index.html at the root, and other software or PDFs. Each project needs a cover image.

Are downloads safe?

Every project is reviewed before it appears publicly and anyone can report a project. As with any software, only install things from creators you trust.
