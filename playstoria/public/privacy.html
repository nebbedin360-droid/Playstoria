Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information Playstoria collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account details: username, email address and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text). If you use Google sign-in, we receive your verified email and basic profile.
  • Content you submit: project files, cover images, descriptions, reviews and reports.
  • Payment proof: for paid apps, the transaction reference and screenshot you upload. These are private and visible only to administrators.
  • Technical data: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and basic server logs for security.

How we use it

  • To run your account, show your projects and process reviews, purchases and payouts.
  • To keep the platform safe, prevent abuse and moderate content.
  • To send essential emails such as email verification.

Sharing

Your username, bio, published projects and reviews are public. We do not sell your personal data. We share information only when required by law or to protect the service and its users.

Cookies

Playstoria uses one essential session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.

Security and retention

We use industry-standard safeguards, but no system is perfectly secure. We keep your data while your account is active and as needed for payment records and legal reasons. You can ask for your account to be deleted.

Your choices

You can edit your bio, delete your own projects, or request deletion of your account by contacting the Playstoria team.
